# Screenshot Index & Capture Guide

For the final assessment portfolio, the following screenshots must be captured from the running application.

## 1. Customer-Facing Website (Marketing)
- `[ ]` **Homepage** (`/`): Capture the Hero section showing "Secure Government ICT Solutions".
- `[ ]` **Solutions** (`/solutions`): Capture the E-Passport and Fintech service offerings.
- `[ ]` **Industries** (`/industries`): Capture the target industry grid.
- `[ ]` **Contact/Quote** (`/contact`): Capture the Lead capture form.

## 2. Authentication & Profile
- `[ ]` **Login Page** (`/login`): Capture the secure authentication portal.
- `[ ]` **User Settings** (`/settings`): Capture the profile management and theme toggles.

## 3. CRM & Sales
- `[ ]` **Main Dashboard** (`/dashboard`): Capture the KPI cards (Revenue, Projects, Corporate Clients).
- `[ ]` **Sales Pipeline** (`/crm/sales`): Capture the Kanban board with Lead columns (New Inquiry, Qualification, etc.).
- `[ ]` **Client Workspace** (`/crm/customers/[id]`): Capture a 360-degree view of a specific client, their timeline, and systems.
- `[ ]` **Clients List** (`/crm/customers`): Capture the main data table showing client statuses.

## 4. Operations & Projects
- `[ ]` **Operations Queue** (`/crm/service`): Capture the inbound service request queue.
- `[ ]` **Project Dashboard** (`/crm/repairs`): Capture the active projects tracking table.
- `[ ]` **Project Workspace** (`/crm/repairs/[id]`): Capture the specific project details, including the checklist and assigned technician.

## 5. Inventory & Supply Chain
- `[ ]` **Inventory Ledger** (`/crm/inventory`): Capture the main inventory list with `currentStock` and `reservedStock`.
- `[ ]` **Stock Movement** (`/crm/inventory/[id]`): Capture the immutable `InventoryMovement` timeline showing deductions and additions.

## 6. Billing & Finance
- `[ ]` **Invoice List** (`/crm/invoices`): Capture the financial dashboard showing DRAFT and ISSUED invoices.
- `[ ]` **Invoice Workspace** (`/crm/invoices/[id]`): Capture an individual invoice with its immutable line items and tax calculations.
- `[ ]` **Payment & Receipts** (`/crm/payments/[id]`): Capture a finalized Payment Allocation screen showing how funds were applied to invoices.
